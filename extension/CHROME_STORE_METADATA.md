## 2. Detailed Store Description (Copy & Paste)

```markdown
PDFBolt is a free, fast, and 100% private PDF utility suite that operates entirely inside your web browser. 

Unlike legacy PDF converters that upload your confidential documents to external cloud servers, PDFBolt processes all documents locally using modern WebAssembly and in-memory Web Workers. Your files never leave your device.

KEY FEATURES & TOOLS:
• Merge PDF: Combine multiple PDF documents into a single organized file with instant drag-and-drop reordering.
• Compress PDF: Reduce PDF file size down to 100KB, 200KB, 500KB, or 1MB with zero quality compromise.
• Split PDF: Extract specific pages or split multi-page documents instantly.
• PDF to Word: Convert PDFs into editable DOCX format preserving original fonts, layouts, and tables.
• Edit & Annotate PDF: Add text, shapes, highlights, and drawings right on your PDF.
• Redact PDF: Permanently remove sensitive PII (Aadhaar, PAN, emails, phone numbers, and bank account numbers).
• Indian Exam Presets: Instant single-click sizing tailored for SSC, UPSC, NEET, JEE, IBPS, and RRB portals.
• Password Protect & Unlock: Secure documents with military-grade AES encryption or unlock protected files.

PRIVACY GUARANTEE:
100% Private & In-Browser. You can test PDFBolt completely offline in Airplane mode. No daily task limits, no email signups, and zero watermarks.

Website: https://pdfbolt.in
```
